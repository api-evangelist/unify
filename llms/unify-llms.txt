# Unify

> Unify is outbound AI for sellers, AI agents and reps working side by side to find the buyers already in market and reach them with the right message.

## Key Facts

- Founded: 2023
- Founders: Austin Hughes (Co-Founder & CEO), Connor Heggie (Co-Founder & CTO)
- Funding: $52M+ from OpenAI Startup Fund, Thrive Capital, Battery Ventures, and Emergence Capital
- HQ: San Francisco Bay Area
- Customers: Perplexity, Together AI, Justworks, Decagon, Juicebox, and hundreds of other fast-growing companies

## Product

- [Agents](https://www.unifygtm.com/product/agents): AI-powered lead research, qualification, and message personalization agents
- [Signals & Intent](https://www.unifygtm.com/products/signals): 25+ B2B intent signals including website visits, product usage, champion tracking, hiring activity, and AI-powered Infinity Signals
- [Plays](https://www.unifygtm.com/product/plays): Workflow builder for automated signal-triggering, prospecting, enrichment, and sequence enrollment
- [Sequencing](https://www.unifygtm.com/product/sequencing): Multi-channel outbound sequence execution across email, calling, and other channels
- [Deliverability](https://www.unifygtm.com/product/deliverability): Managed mailbox infrastructure and deliverability monitoring for outbound sending
- [B2B Contact Data](https://www.unifygtm.com/product/b2b-company-contact-data): Access more than 1.1B+ contacts, 65M+ companies, and more than 40+ signal and intent data sources. Includes providers like Prospeo, ContactOut, Wiza, Forager, and Findymail.
- [Analytics](https://www.unifygtm.com/product/analytics): Pipeline attribution and outbound performance reporting

## Pricing

- [Pricing](https://www.unifygtm.com/pricing): Free plan at $0. Base at $20/seat/month, Pro at $60/seat/month (most popular, 14-day free trial), and Business with custom annual pricing. Credit-based system for data and actions.
